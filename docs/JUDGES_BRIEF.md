# MVA Safe Rescue: one-page brief

Track 2 entry for Rare Disease, Real Kid: the 2026 MVA Hackathon.

## Decision

Investigate arimoclomol and elamipretide in a qualified neural model, and only
if the authorized case analysis supports the selected checkpoint mechanism.
Neither has been shown to work in MVA. Sirolimus, everolimus and acetylcysteine
are controls and cannot advance.

## The problem

A compound can keep abnormal cells alive without restoring functional neurons.
The published rapamycin result in the fly model shows exactly that. Our proposed
experiment requires neural output and two mitochondrial benefits at both locked
doses. A chromosome or oncology safety failure vetoes advancement, and no amount
of benefit cancels it.

## What we found

The primary fly analysis has 179 gene-level calls and 0 of 12 supported modules.
A separately analyzed public mouse dataset adds 23 samples and a predeclared
mechanism panel of 84 tests. Seventeen tests meet the fixed thresholds, and the
patterns depend on tissue and genotype. The panel gives no coherent support for
HSP70 or cardiolipin. It contains no drug-response experiment and does not
override the fly null.

## What the entry adds

The decisions are executable and list the evidence that is still missing. They
come with positive and negative synthetic controls, an independent public mouse
analysis, exact R dependency locks, complete evidence tables and a short pitch.
The implementation can be reused. The biological rules need to be qualified
again for each new mechanism and model.

## The next result that matters

Both eligible candidates against vehicle, on one neural and two mitochondrial
endpoints at two doses, with every safety gate and independent confirmation.
Both candidates may fail. We have not engaged a lab, obtained a quote or
collected response data.
